## 4. Rate Negotiation Counters (Protecting Agency Margins)

### Scenario A: Creator rate is 2x–3x above agency budget
*Creator: "My standard rate for a dedicated TikTok is £600." (Budget: £250)*

```text
"Thanks for sharing your rate, [Creator Name]! We love your content quality, but our opening test budget for this initial campaign is £250 base. 

However, because we're confident your audience will convert, we can offer:
1. £250 guaranteed base fee
2. An upgraded 20% affiliate commission on every sale generated through your link/showcase
3. Up to £1,000 in Spark Ads paid ad spend behind your video (which brings you massive profile traffic)
4. A guaranteed monthly retainer contract if this first video hits our benchmark!

Would you be open to testing this initial collaboration structure?"
```

### Scenario B: Gifting to Paid Transition
*Creator: "I only do paid collaborations, I don't accept gifted products."*

```text
"Totally understand and respect that, [Creator Name]! We always pay our creators for contracted deliverables. 

We’d love to send you the product first with zero posting obligation so you can test it, verify the premium quality for yourself, and make sure it’s an authentic fit for your audience. Once it arrives and you love it, we can lock in the paid contract and creative brief. 

What’s the best address to send your VIP test box?"
```

## 5. Creator Guide: How to Generate a TikTok 365-Day Spark Ad Code

*Copy-paste this exact step-by-step instruction sheet to creators when requesting Spark codes.*

```text
Hey [Creator Name]! Here is how to generate your TikTok Spark Ads code in 30 seconds:

1. Open your published video on TikTok.
2. Tap the three dots (...) on the right side of the screen.
3. Scroll right on the bottom menu and tap "Ad settings".
4. Toggle ON "Ad authorization".
5. Tap "Video code" -> Tap "Generate".
6. Select authorization period: Select "365 days".
7. Tap "Authorize" -> Tap "Copy code" and paste it into our chat!

That's it! Thank you for partnering with Fairytales SMM! ✨
```
