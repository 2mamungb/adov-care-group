# Fairytales SMM: Holiday UGC Creative Briefs & Viral Scripts

Use these standardized UGC scripts to direct creators for fast-turnaround Christmas campaigns (both for in-house products and external agency clients).

---

## Concept 1: "The Problem Solver / Ultimate Christmas Gift"

**Visual Style:** Casual bedroom or living room with subtle holiday decor (fairy lights, small tree in background).  
**Tone:** Excited, helpful, authentic friend sharing a secret find.

```
[0:00 - 0:03] HOOK VARIATIONS (Film all 3):
- Hook A: "If you're impossible to buy for or don't know what to get your [mom/partner/friend] this Christmas, stop scrolling."
- Hook B: "This is hands-down the best £30 I’ve spent on Christmas gifts this year."
- Hook C: "POV: You found the one gift everyone will actually fight over at Christmas."

[0:03 - 0:15] UNBOXING & REACTION:
- Creator opens the gift box, showing high-quality festive packaging.
- "I was struggling with gift ideas, and then I found [Product Name]. Look at the quality on this..."

[0:15 - 0:28] VALUE & DEMONSTRATION:
- Cut to 3 quick B-roll shots of the product in action.
- "It does [Feature 1], [Feature 2], and the best part is [Key Benefit/Ease of Use]."

[0:28 - 0:35] CHRISTMAS URGENCY & CTA:
- "Their holiday shipping cut-off is coming up, and stock is already low. Tap the link/orange cart below before it sells out!"
```

## Concept 2: "The Honest Review & Stocking Stuffer Hack"

**Visual Style:** Fast-paced cuts, front-facing camera, text on screen.  
**Tone:** Direct, punchy, curiosity-driven.

```
[0:00 - 0:03] HOOK VARIATIONS:
- Hook A: "Stop buying boring socks for Christmas. Get this instead."
- Hook B: "The #1 stocking stuffer on TikTok right now, tested and reviewed."
- Hook C: "Why is nobody talking about this Christmas hack?"

[0:03 - 0:18] COMPARISON / PAIN POINT:
- Show traditional generic gift vs. [Product Name].
- "Normally, gifts in this category cost £60+, feel cheap, and end up in a drawer by January."

[0:18 - 0:32] PRODUCT PROOF:
- Creator demonstrates the exact feature that makes it special.
- "This actually [Solves specific problem], feels super premium, and costs under [Price Point]."

[0:32 - 0:40] CALL TO ACTION:
- "Order today to make sure it arrives in time for Christmas with guaranteed standard shipping!"
```

## Concept 3: "Early Gift Reaction / The Authentic Unboxing"

**Visual Style:** Two-person interaction or self-gifting reaction.  
**Tone:** Heartwarming, genuine emotional reaction.

```
[0:00 - 0:03] HOOK VARIATIONS:
- Hook A: "I gave my [partner/bestie] their Christmas gift early and this was their honest reaction..."
- Hook B: "Do NOT open this in front of anyone who gets jealous easily."
- Hook C: "Christmas came early this year..."

[0:03 - 0:20] EMOTION & SURPRISE:
- Handing over the wrapped gift or unwrapping.
- Genuine smile, gasping at the design/material.

[0:20 - 0:35] DETAILED SHOWCASE:
- "They've been wanting one of these for months. It feels so luxurious and the packaging is 10/10."

[0:35 - 0:45] CALL TO ACTION:
- "Check the link below for their holiday discount bundle—you get 20% off when you buy 2!"
```
