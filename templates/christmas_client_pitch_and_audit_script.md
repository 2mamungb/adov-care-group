## 3. The 60-Second Loom Video Audit Script

**Step 1: Screen share the client's current Meta Ad Library or TikTok profile (0:00 - 0:15)**  
*"Hey [First Name], I’m recording this quick video for you because I saw your active ads for [Product Name]. Your product looks incredible, but right now, your creatives are still using evergreen angles."*

**Step 2: Show the Holiday Opportunity (0:15 - 0:35)**  
*"Between November 1st and December 20th, over 70% of shoppers are buying for someone else, not themselves. If your ads don't immediately hook them with 'The #1 Christmas Gift for [Boyfriend / Mom / Tech Lovers]', your conversion rate drops."*

**Step 3: Present the 3 Custom Hooks (0:35 - 0:50)**  
*"Here are 3 hooks we wrote specifically for your product:  
1. 'If you're stuck on what to get [Avatar] for Christmas, stop scrolling...'  
2. 'The viral stocking stuffer that’s selling out before Black Friday...'  
3. 'I ordered 5 Christmas gifts early—this one completely blew me away.' "*

**Step 4: The Frictionless Call to Action (0:50 - 1:00)**  
*"We can produce all 3 of these with our vetted creator roster in under 7 days. If you’d like to see the full creative brief, grab 10 minutes on my link below. Have a great Q4!"*
