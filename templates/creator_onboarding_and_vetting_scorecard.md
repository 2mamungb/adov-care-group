## 3. Decision Matrix

- **Score 85 - 100: VIP Tier 1 (Paid Retainer + Seed)**
  - Fast-track for paid collaboration, affiliate partnership, and custom PR box.
- **Score 70 - 84: Tier 2 (Product Seeding / Affiliate Only)**
  - Send complimentary product gift box + offer 15-20% TikTok Shop affiliate commission. Upgrade to paid upon proof of performance.
- **Score Below 70: Decline**
  - Do not gift product or deploy agency budget.
