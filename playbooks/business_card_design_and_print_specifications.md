## 2. Cinema Studio Mockup & PDF Specifications

- **Presentation Canvas**: 2752 × 1536 px (16:9 Cinema 4K Display Resolution).
- **Physical Setting**: Authentic rustic aged walnut timber tabletop with organic wood grain, knots, and warm amber tones.
- **Lighting Setup**: Angled top-left dramatic studio key spotlight with natural inverse-square falloff and soft edge vignetting.
- **Card Placement**:
  - **Left Card**: Executive Obverse (Porcelain White Front) placed at -3.5° physical tilt.
  - **Right Card**: Brand Reverse (Obsidian Noir Back) placed at +3.5° physical tilt.
- **Shadow Simulation**:
  - Multi-tiered realistic contact shadows: tight ambient occlusion under card edges + soft directional keylight shadow + distant ambient bounce shadow.
  - 3D card core thickness (0.76 mm credit card depth) with subtle edge bevel highlight.
  - Subtle overhead softbox specular sheen across the card surfaces.
