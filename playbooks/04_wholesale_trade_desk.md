# B2B Wholesale & Trade Desk Launch Playbook

Opening a dedicated B2B Wholesale Desk unlocks high-volume, recurring cash flow with low marketing overhead and predictable forecasting.

---

## 1. Wholesale Pricing & Policy Framework

To prevent wholesale buyers from cannibalizing your retail Amazon and DTC sales, strict pricing governance is essential.

| Pricing Tier | Definition | Standard Discount | Terms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MSRP / RRP** | Recommended Retail Price | Baseline (100%) | Direct consumers on DTC / Amazon |
| **MAP** | Minimum Advertised Price | Mandatory floor | Legally binding minimum price buyers may display online |
| **Tier 1: Boutique / Indie** | Small retailers ($500 - $2,500 MOQ) | 40% - 45% off MSRP | Proforma (100% upfront) |
| **Tier 2: Regional Distributor**| Multi-store accounts ($2,500 - $10,000 MOQ) | 50% off MSRP | 50% deposit, 50% pre-dispatch or Net 30 |
| **Tier 3: Key National Accounts**| Chains & Department Stores ($10,000+ MOQ) | 55% - 60% off MSRP | Net 30 / Net 60 (Credit Insurance / Factoring) |

> [!CAUTION]
> **Strict MAP Policy**: Every wholesale account MUST sign a **MAP Agreement** prohibiting unauthorized resale on Amazon, TikTok Shop, or Temu. This prevents price wars and Buy Box suppression.

## 2. Infrastructure & Digital Line Sheets

1. **Wholesale Digital Portal**:
   - Deploy **Shopify Plus B2B** or **Wholesale Gorilla** app on your primary site to allow wholesale buyers to log in, view tier-specific wholesale pricing, and place wholesale orders using purchase orders or trade credit.
2. **Interactive Digital Line Sheet (PDF / NuORDER / Brandboom)**:
   - Cover Page: Brand story, key USPs, customer reviews, press features.
   - Product Grid: SKU code, EAN/barcode, wholesale cost, MSRP, MOQ, case pack qty, carton dimensions, and lead times.
   - Terms & Conditions: Carriage terms (Ex-Works / FOB / DAP), carriage-paid thresholds (e.g., Free freight on orders over £500/$750).
3. **Wholesale Discovery Marketplaces**:
   - **Faire**: Access to 100,000+ independent retail shops globally. Offer Net 60 terms funded by Faire with zero credit risk to you.
   - **Ankorstore**: Leading European wholesale marketplace.
   - **RangeMe**: The standard discovery platform for big-box retail buyers (Target, Walmart, Tesco, B&Q, Boots).

- Use **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** to find:
  - "Category Buyer", "Merchant", "Procurement Manager", "Retail Store Owner" within your specific industry vertical.
