## 2. UGC Production Framework: The 3-Second Conversion Formula

Every piece of UGC content produced by Fairytales SMM follows our proven performance formula:

```
[0:00 - 0:03] THE HOOK (Pattern Interrupt)
- Visual Shock / Unexpected Action / Text on screen with voiceover
- Examples: 
  * "Stop scrolling if you struggle with [Problem]..."
  * "TikTok made me buy it, but is it actually worth it?"
  * "The secret hack [Competitor] doesn't want you to know..."

[0:03 - 0:15] THE AGITATION & DISCOVERY
- Relatable demonstration of the pain point
- Seamless introduction of your hero product as the effortless solution

[0:15 - 0:30] SOCIAL PROOF & KEY BENEFITS
- Demonstrating the product in real-world application
- Highlight 2-3 tangible features that directly solve the pain point
- Address top 2 consumer objections (durability, ease of use, price)

[0:30 - 0:45] IRRESISTIBLE CALL TO ACTION (CTA)
- Urgency: "Click the yellow shopping bag below before flash sale stock sells out!"
- Discount / Guarantee: "Use code FAIRYTALE15 for 15% off + 30-day money-back guarantee."
```

## 3. Agency Client Service Retainers (For External Businesses)

Fairytales SMM packages services into three high-margin monthly retainers:

### Tier 1: The UGC Creative Suite (£1,750 - £2,500 / month)
- **Target**: Brands with existing media buyers who need high-converting creative ad variations.
- **Deliverables**:
  - 8-10 professionally scripted and edited UGC videos per month.
  - 3 hook variations per video (24-30 total testable creatives).
  - Full commercial licensing & Spark Ads / Meta Whitelisting rights for 90 days.

### Tier 2: Influencer Seeding & TikTok Shop Growth (£3,500 - £5,000 / month)
- **Target**: E-commerce brands wanting organic viral reach and creator affiliate volume.
- **Deliverables**:
  - Direct outreach and negotiation with 100 targeted creators/month.
  - 25-35 confirmed creator sample seedings and posted videos/month.
  - TikTok Shop Affiliate Center management & commission tracking.
  - Bi-weekly performance review and analytics reporting.

### Tier 3: Full-Funnel Omnipresence Retainer (£6,500 - £10,000 / month + % of Ad Spend)
- **Target**: Established brands looking to outsource complete social acquisition.
- **Deliverables**:
  - Everything in Tier 1 and Tier 2.
  - Celebrity / Macro-influencer negotiations and brand ambassadorship coordination.
  - Paid Social Media Buying across TikTok Ads Manager, Meta Ads (IG/FB), and Google/YouTube.
  - Dedicated Account Director and 24/7 Slack channel support.
